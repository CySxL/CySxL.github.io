## 2.1.6
* move to the com.cysxl package id, replacing atar13's Vaon.

Changes from atar13's Vaon 1.2.0:
* support iOS 16 on roothide and rootless, and drop iOS 13 to 15.
* drop the iPad style grid app switcher.
* list speakers, car stereos and other devices with no battery reading, with an infinity sign and a pulsing ring.
* add Pinned Devices, reorderable and with a custom glyph from 71 symbols.
* add a paged layout with page dots and a set number of devices per page.
* add Landscape options to hide Vaon or move it clear of a floating dock, and a Side Module for landscape.
* apply settings on the next switcher open, without a respring, except Enabled and Module.
* pick colors with the system color picker, and double tap a slider to type a value.
* keep one record per accessory instead of one per connection.
* fix the brief left aligned view when the switcher opens, and the home gesture near the module.
