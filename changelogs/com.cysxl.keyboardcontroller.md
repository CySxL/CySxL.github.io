## 1.5.6
* move to the com.cysxl package id, replacing Tomasz Poliszuk's Keyboard Controller.

Changes from Tomasz Poliszuk's Keyboard Controller 1.2.3:
* support iOS 15 and 16 on roothide and rootless.
* turn the one-handed keyboard on & off with a swipe down on the 123, delete or return key.
* add One-Handed Tools: cursor, copy, paste and select all keys in the space beside a one-handed keyboard.
* turn off emoji skin tone variants when that option is off.
* leave horizontal swipes to SwipeSelection.
* fix settings not loading in sandboxed apps.
* show a change on the settings page's test fields at once.
* show the version and build scheme in a settings header.
