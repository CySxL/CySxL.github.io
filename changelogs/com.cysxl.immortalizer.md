## 2.8.3
* move to the com.cysxl package id, replacing sergy's Immortalizer.

Changes from sergy's Immortalizer 2.2:
* support roothide and rootless.
* hold only an immortalized app's own scene in the foreground.
* stop Notification Center and Control Center pausing apps that are not immortalized.
* fix an app freezing when immortalized from Control Center while paused by it.
* fix an app opened straight into a Myrtle window being killed a second later.
* show whether an immortalized app is running or stopped in the state indicator.
* clear the indicators and lock marks at once when their switch is turned off.
* keep the toast centered in landscape and visible while an app is closed from the switcher.
* flip the Control Center tile glyph when an app is immortalized.
* keep the immortalized and locked lists in the tweak's own preferences.
