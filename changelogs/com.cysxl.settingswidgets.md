## 2.0.14
* move to the com.cysxl package id, replacing shepgoba's SettingsWidgets.

Changes from shepgoba's SettingsWidgets 1.2.3:
* support roothide and rootless.
* add Bluetooth, hotspot and utility widgets.
* respring or enter safe mode from the utility widget.
* show hotspot clients from HotspotPro.
* set an image or GIF background per widget, with a button to clear unused ones.
* open a widget's settings pane with a tap.
* show cellular usage in the units the other widgets use.
* follow dark mode in the storage bar.
* hide the rows of disabled widgets and of switches that are off.
* make sliders continuous, with a double tap to type a value.
